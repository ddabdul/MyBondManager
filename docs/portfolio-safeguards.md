# Portfolio safeguards

QuantLib 1.44 supplies native schedule/calendar and coupon calculations. The app supports reviewed EUR fixed-rate annual, semiannual and quarterly schedules, zero-coupon redemption, and explicitly configured plain EURIBOR floating or fixed-to-floating forecasts using frozen dated market inputs. Reviewed fixed-rate and zero-coupon holdings use QuantLib to solve their Estimated YTM at purchase from dated cash payments.

## Purchase yield

Estimated YTM is the annual effective return at purchase, solved with QuantLib from the clean acquisition cost plus accrued interest and all entitled coupon/redemption payments on their TARGET-adjusted payment days. Accrued interest uses the reviewed Actual/Actual ICMA coupon schedule, including stub reference periods; discounting uses Actual/365 Fixed calendar days and annual compounding. The acquisition date is the valuation date (no separate settlement date is stored). Clean cost is not rewritten and fees are not invented. This investment return can differ from a broker's quoted market YTM, and it does not update with market prices or the current forecast cutoff. Callable holdings still assume redemption at contractual maturity.

Coupon entitlement keeps the existing contractual-date-on-or-after-acquisition rule. An entitled coupon on the acquisition day has its full accrued amount added to the initial outlay and its receipt netted by the yield solver, avoiding a free coupon. Payment delay adds no accrual. Yield is unavailable when there is no future positive payment or unknown historical opening coupons prevent reconstructing the purchase's full entitled schedule. The latter can still have a valid future forecast. Floating and fixed-to-floating holdings retain unavailable YTM. Missing yields are excluded from the portfolio's nominal-weighted average, which remains an average of purchase yields.

Saved derived yields are recalculated when terms/projections are applied; display and export derive the current result from approved terms without rewriting clean cost or stored history. Native sidecars mark approved entries with `yieldMethod: annualEffectiveActual365FixedCleanPrice`. Older unmarked native exports are checked against their original approximation; the prior `annualEffectiveActual365Fixed` marker is checked against the former cash-outlay solver. Both are recalculated from clean cost on import. Unknown conventions or inconsistent yields are rejected. The legacy `bonds.json` keys remain unchanged; old app versions may reject a new native export because they do not understand the corrected convention. The standalone quick calculator remains explicitly approximate because it has no reviewed payment schedule.

## First review and subsequent refreshes

The additive V3 migration retains positions, existing cash-flow rows and financial history. Existing bonds initially need review: their invested capital remains visible, while unverified payments are omitted from forecasts and automatic recording. No ING collection runs during schema migration or normal launch.

Open **Review Terms**, select unresolved holdings, and choose **Collect missing terms**. The review list shows each normalized ISIN once, with its purchase count. Each selected ISIN is fetched once per collection batch. Instrument draft edits and approval apply to every purchase of that ISIN; purchase costs, acquisition dates, banks, schedules and yields remain separate. Successful drafts survive another ISIN's failure and resume after reopening. Collect missing terms skips saved drafts; **Refresh selected from ING** explicitly requests another collection. Confirmed overrides and known unsupported restrictions from every purchase are retained. Identical repeated clipboard ISIN lines resolve to one lookup identifier; distinct ISINs pasted together are rejected with an actionable message. Stored position identifiers and history are not rewritten. If Apply has no draft for its representative purchase, it uses a saved sibling draft; otherwise its error retains the latest collection failure. Manual entry preserves existing saved drafts. Conflicting confirmed values stop collection for that ISIN without replacing its saved drafts; use an explicit shared draft save to resolve them.

Inspect current versus proposed coupon/maturity, the acquisition-day interpretation, terms and dated payments. Correct fields as needed, choose **Save draft and preview**, then **Apply Selected ISINs**. Missing values remain missing. An incomplete holding can remain Needs review; no annual frequency or callable status is invented. A changed holding or a preview spanning midnight requires a fresh preview. Each ISIN uses its own isolated transaction covering all its purchases. Missing terms, stale previews or a failed save for one ISIN do not roll back successful instruments. Conflicting saved drafts for the same ISIN require an explicit shared draft save before approval. Successful ISINs are deselected; failed ones and their drafts remain selected in the open review, with a per-ISIN error summary.

**Reuse approved terms** explicitly shares an existing approval with other purchases of the selected ISINs, without fetching ING. The latest approved terms are used; conflicting approvals with the same timestamp require review. Saved confirmed draft changes, including convention-only edits, are retained without being approved. Reuse cannot bypass newly collected restrictions, deferral provisions or issuer-verified first-coupon changes absent from an old approval. Opening the review from either the main toolbar or Edit Bond only loads saved drafts. App startup and imports do not automatically propagate approvals.

New entry can save a holding as Needs review once its required purchase/coupon/maturity values are supplied, or save reviewed supported terms with approval. Saving a new purchase of an already approved ISIN reuses the approved terms for that new purchase, with a fresh prospective activation boundary. Ordinary trade edits with no protected history invalidate that purchase's readiness and retain both the saved instrument approval and an unapproved working draft. Explicit reuse rebuilds its schedule and yield from the saved approval, even if it is the only purchase. Changes to ISIN, coupon or maturity invalidate the saved approval for that purchase. Instrument changes with related history remain blocked.

## Supported terms and conventions

Readiness requires validated EUR terms, a supported structure, finite financial values, positive redemption percentage and known callable status. Fixed schedules also require supported frequency and a consistent next-coupon checkpoint on the maturity-anchored regular grid. An aged checkpoint is compared with its original observation day. An off-grid historical accrual start does not block the remaining regular coupons: the first two potential opening coupon dates are excluded to avoid pricing a short or long initial period without its terms. Approval is allowed only when the checkpoint is later than those dates and neither opening payment, including any TARGET delay, would enter this purchase's preview. The preview displays this limitation. With a confirmed first-coupon date, the actual long or short opening period is calculated using Actual/Actual (ICMA) reference periods and later coupons retain their regular amounts. [Instrument terms](verified-bond-terms.md) are supplied by ING or entered and confirmed in the shared review; no instrument-specific supplement is bundled. An upcoming irregular opening coupon needs a confirmed first-coupon date; its amount is prorated from accrual start, never assumed to be a full regular coupon. The first-coupon date is editable instrument data shared once per ISIN. Issue and accrual-start dates are preserved separately. A schedule exceeding 1,200 periods has a separate range error. Ambiguous month-end rolls and other irregular periods need review; indexed, perpetual and amortizing structures remain unsupported. Floating schedules use the separately confirmed reset and ACT/360 contract described in [market data](quantlib-market-data.md).

The approved defaults are Actual/Actual (ICMA), TARGET and Following, each marked assumed until confirmed. These are application defaults, not universal terms for every Eurobond. Other conventions can be retained explicitly, but do not produce Ready forecasts in this version. Known unsupported provider restrictions cannot be erased by changing a structure label.

Contractual accrual boundaries remain unadjusted. Coupons for a regular period equal nominal times annual coupon rate divided by frequency; buying within the period does not prorate the next coupon by ownership days. Payment delay adds no interest. Redemption equals nominal times redemption percentage divided by 100. Zero-coupon bonds produce no coupon events, regardless of incidental provider frequency/date fields. Callable bonds use contractual maturity with an **Early redemption is not modeled** warning. Known deferrable payments are conditional forecasts with a warning; automatic coupon and redemption receipt recording is disabled. Floating and fixed-to-floating instruments require explicit reset terms and a matching frozen market snapshot; unsupported product shapes retain specific review reasons. Published floating interest can be positive or negative. Future projected interest is never automatically recorded as a receipt. Reconciliation scans from the original activation boundary so a late fixing can recover an unpaid event after the global launch checkpoint has advanced; existing event keys and aliases prevent duplicates. Corrected fixings that disagree with recorded receipts require history review and never rewrite those receipts.

## Forecasts, dates and financial history

Instrument days use explicit calendar dates and Europe/Berlin payment instants. TARGET weekends/holidays move payments forward. Contractual maturity and redemption payment are distinct: an approved holding remains active through its adjusted redemption day, including a delayed final coupon. Cash-flow filters include whole selected market days. Next Coupon and Next Redemption use approved validated payments; missing yield estimates are excluded from the weighted yield.

Unresolved holdings remain visible with their capital and exclusion count. Grouped holdings retain every purchase and indicate mixed readiness. Legacy unverified rows remain stored but are not forecast. Analytical expected-profit and signed-loss rows are not additional cash receipts.

Terms approval/recalculation preserves past-day cash-flow values and every recorded historical row. Trade nominal, cost, acquisition instant and bank are not rewritten by terms approval. Ordinary edits to instrument identity, financial values, dates or bank are blocked when related keyed or ambiguous unlinked bond history exists; descriptive corrections remain available.

Economic payment keys use holding UUID, event kind and contractual day. Safe aliases retain exact local legacy keys before projections/import replacement; old rows and keys are not rewritten. Recalculation retains approval and activation. Approval or native import activates automatic recording prospectively at the actual local operation instant: past and earlier-today payments are never claimed as newly received. A failed event save cannot advance the launch checkpoint.

Ambiguous unlinked legacy receipts overlapping a revised payment window require history review rather than automatic attribution or a possible duplicate. Historical reconciliation remains a separate feature; this phase does not repair old records.

## Cash-flow calendar

Cash Flows separates Upcoming from Past report. Upcoming List and Calendar share the same validated canonical receipts and bank, search, payment-type and date filters: coupons plus full contractual repayment, with gain/loss shown only as purchase information and never counted twice. Both default to All future payments; custom Upcoming dates cannot precede today. It reads validated approved payment schedules without changing stored projections, history or export formats.

Years shows compact clickable annual tiles in a responsive grid, with total receipts, coupons and repayments and no month calendars; selecting a year opens its twelve month calendars and month totals; Month shows a Monday-first day grid and native payment-day popovers grouped by ISIN with individual purchase breakdowns. Navigation uses adjusted payment dates and reveals contractual dates in details. Coupons, repayments, bank/search and an optional date range filter the calendar; All future payments is the default. Calendar mode and selected period survive main-tab switches during the session.

Projected and conditional payments are labeled, and canonical schedule warnings (including call assumptions and stale frozen market data) remain available in details. Active holdings whose approvals cannot be validated are excluded with their reasons and a Review terms action. Empty periods show explicit no-payment information; archived invalid records do not inflate active exclusions.

## Import review preservation

Both legacy import modes preserve local approved terms, saved drafts, activation boundaries and generated cash-flow provenance when the same holding UUID has unchanged ISIN, nominal, acquisition cost, coupon, bank and dates. Descriptive fields may update; imported legacy cash flows and derived yields do not overwrite the reviewed schedule. Dates differing only by the subsecond precision omitted by legacy JSON retain their original stored instants. A native entry without approval also retains the matching local review. Identical approved native terms and frozen market context keep the local approval, activation and generated rows. Existing local drafts take priority; an incoming native draft is restored when no local draft exists. Descriptive updates refresh the saved draft baseline so reopening it remains usable, while reviews already open still reject stale changes.

A change that would discard saved review data rejects the entire transaction with an actionable error, unless a validated native entry supplies approved replacement terms. The completion count reflects active holdings still needing review after import. Explicit category replacement still removes omitted positions as stated in its confirmation. These safeguards prevent future loss; they do not reconstruct review data already missing from the database.

## ETF transaction dates and deletion

ETF behavior is unchanged. Enter purchases and sales in chronological local-day order per ISIN. Recorded keyed sale cutoffs survive deletion/import; older unlinked sales conservatively protect all ETFs. Same-day transactions are allowed. Daily ETF history stores the latest successful quote for that local day, and sale history uses entered execution price/date.

Bond deletion is isolated and atomic, cascades projected rows and preserves history. A failed save leaves persisted data intact. Current-position transfers are not history backups; see [positions-transfer.md](positions-transfer.md).

## iPhone payment alerts

Cash Flows > iPhone alerts enables an opt-in EventKit connection to a dedicated **Bond payments** calendar in the selected iCloud account. Enable iCloud Calendar on the Mac and iPhone using the same Apple Account, then enable Calendar notifications on the phone. Full Calendar permission is required to reconcile existing events; the sandbox entitlement and privacy purpose string are included. The app offers CalDAV accounts (which include iCloud) and requires explicit selection; it does not silently select a local calendar. Calendar permission is requested only through the settings action, never on startup.

Saved portfolio changes (including background imports and approvals), launch, foreground activation and a new payment day trigger coalesced automatic sync while the app runs. All approved future payments are included, independent of the on-screen search, bank or date filters. Purchases sharing normalized ISIN and adjusted payment day produce one event. Coupons and full redemption are separate in event details; capital gains are not added again. Events are expected-payment reminders, never confirmation that a bank has credited funds. Projected, conditional and canonical warning text is retained.

Only events bearing the app payment URL marker inside the persisted dedicated calendar are reconciled. Matching events are updated, duplicate marked events removed, and stale future events removed for deleted/unapproved holdings. Past events and unmarked user events are retained. Unexpected saved-profile decoding/calculation errors abort the sync before touching Calendar. Missing/read-only calendars, permission denial and write failures remain visible through the toolbar warning and settings until a successful sync. A disappeared calendar can be replaced by stopping sync and enabling it again.

Events are all-day calendar entries, with absolute alarms at09:00 by default in the Mac time zone at synchronization. Alert hour, an optional day-before alarm and amount-free event titles are configurable. Amounts remain in event details even when hidden from titles. Already-synced iPhone alerts do not require the Mac to remain on; revised dates/amounts need a later app run to publish. Stopping sync leaves existing events and alerts intact; delete the dedicated calendar in Apple Calendar to remove them. No hosted sender, SMS/email integration, portfolio schema migration, or changes to financial history are involved.

Apple references: [EventKit access and sandbox permissions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/eventkit/accessing-the-event-store), [iCloud Calendar across devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-calendar-mme4d73a8727/icloud).

Calendar change notifications are deliberately not synchronization triggers: EventKit emits them for the app's own writes, which can cause a repeated-sync loop. Direct Calendar edits are reconciled on the next portfolio save, launch, foreground activation, day change, settings change or manual sync.

## Tab responsiveness

Saved bond profiles and validated schedule previews use bounded, lock-protected in-memory caches. Decoded profiles are keyed by their exact persisted bytes and contain immutable values only, so unchanged summary tiles and table rows do not repeatedly decode the same JSON. Readiness, current trade fields, exact acquisition instant, coupon and maturity are still checked against the live holding on every validated read. Changed bytes are decoded again; decoding failures are not cached. No managed objects, derived display totals or live holdings are cached.

## Past expected payment reports

In Cash Flows, select **Past report** and choose inclusive From/To dates before today. The report reconstructs expected coupons and full redemptions from current approved schedules for holdings whose contractual maturity is today or later. Bonds that matured before today are omitted from payments, totals, exclusion warnings and JSON, even when they paid within the requested period. It uses adjusted payment dates and purchase entitlement, respects the shared bank, bond-name and payment-type filters, and does not modify portfolio records, financial history or future cash flows. The cutoff uses contractual maturity, so a business-day delay does not retain a bond that has already matured.

Calculations and JSON encoding run on a private Core Data context. Saved holding changes refresh the report. **Download JSON** exports the displayed report snapshot, with a dated range filename; opening the save panel freezes its data even if the portfolio changes afterward. Version 1 includes the inclusive range, generation timestamp, filters, EUR coupon/redemption/total amounts, individual payments with ISIN, purchase ID, bank, contractual/payment dates, rate provenance, conditional status and warnings, and excluded holdings with reasons. `isComplete` is false when relevant holdings cannot be calculated. Exclusions also appear in the app.

This is an expected-payment report, not confirmation that the bank credited a payment. It uses current saved terms and frozen market data where applicable; it cannot recover deleted holdings or earlier versions of terms. Missing approvals and unsupported schedules remain explicit exclusions, and projected or conditional payments remain labeled. Returning to Upcoming restores the previous date range and List/Calendar selection.

The past report's **Review excluded instruments** action opens the existing review scoped to the excluded ISINs, with all purchases of each ISIN. Matured holdings omitted by the report do not generate review warnings. All report instruments start selected. Status, unresolved selection, missing-term collection and approval/reuse/forecast-rebuild validation use the historical report start day; the normal review continues to use current-forecast readiness. The historical failure is shown beside an explicit Edit saved terms action, which stages an editable draft while preserving the saved approval. Drafts show payments for the selected report range. Historical validation failures roll back only their ISIN transaction and remain open. Ordinary portfolio review keeps its active-instrument scope. Exclusion details distinguish missing approvals, changed purchase fields, retained approvals needing rebuilt previews, unsupported historical schedules and unreadable saved approval data. A usable approved sibling produces an explicit reuse suggestion. Opening the review changes no approval; collection, reuse and approval require their existing explicit actions. Successful bulk ISINs save independently, failures remain open, and saving or dismissing the review refreshes the past report.

## Cash-flow presentation

The section reuses the portfolio grey panel, full-width purple-blue title header, rounded large-title typography and alternating compact table surfaces. Calendar year tiles use the same gradient as portfolio summary tiles. Aligned tabular amounts and a shared Expected receipts/Coupons/Repayments summary keep totals consistent across presentations. List years and months have explicit disclosure controls; receipt rows expand to show ISIN, contractual date, gain/loss and calculation caveats. Year tiles still drill into twelve-month and daily calendar views. Day cells show a readable total and payment count; precise payments remain available in day details. Incomplete reports show a concise holdings count with a Review action and a bounded, scrollable explanation list. Projections, conditional payments and routine assumptions use separate labels. The Summary toolbar button hides only the cash-flow portfolio sidebar; Bank remains available in the section filters. Past report retains the maturing-today-or-later scope and its unchanged JSON snapshot/schema.

## Reporting

Reporting is read-only and shares the Depot Bank selection with Portfolio and Cash Flows. It includes active bond purchases, including unreviewed nominal exposure; ETFs and holdings whose redemption date has passed are excluded. Missing or invalid nominal values are excluded explicitly with a visible explanation.

Maturity Schedule groups nominal principal by contractual maturity year and shows each year's share of selected nominal. Purchase YTM is weighted by nominal across holdings with validated yields; partial coverage is marked with an asterisk and unavailable yields display a dash. Issuer Breakdown combines all purchases using their saved issuer names, sorted by descending nominal; shares use the same selected principal denominator.

Expected Interest by Year uses canonical approved coupon receipts grouped by adjusted payment year, including zero-payment years between today and the final coupon. The current year contains only payments on or after the report date. Projected, conditional and saved-assumption warnings remain accessible in Forecast details. The annual target starts at EUR 150,000, is editable and persists as a local preference. Negative or non-finite target values show an error and omit the reference line; non-numeric edits do not replace the previous numeric value. No stored bond terms, approvals, cash flows or export formats are changed by reporting.
